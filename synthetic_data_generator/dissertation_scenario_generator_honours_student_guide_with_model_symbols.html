How the generator maps to the mathematical formulation

Sets

Directly exported notation

D, N, E, A, A_NS, P_STOCKYARD, M_OPERATIONS, M_a, H, W_m, W_h, K.

Daily asset and arc capacity, tonnage and footprint stock bounds, overflow penalties, mode duration, retained capacity, capacity loss, minimum throughput, X1 route and zone compatibility, activity capacity profile sigma[a,d], and incompatibility h[a,d].

Ordinary products versus X1

Ordinary products

P1, P2 and P3

Ordinary products use their normal compatible zones and arcs. They keep the generated grade specifications and do not activate protected campaign commitments by default.

Special product

X1 = Special Product X1

X1 is first-class data: SPECIAL_PRODUCTS = ["X1"], a dedicated SpecialProductRequirements record, Z4-only compatibility, a route through the existing terminal network, non-blending, segregation and aggregate footprint checks.
